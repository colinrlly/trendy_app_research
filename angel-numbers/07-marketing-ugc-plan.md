# 07: Marketing and UGC Plan

## Strategy

Drive installs with short-form UGC on TikTok and Instagram Reels, then convert with the quiz → commitment → hard paywall funnel (`03`). The builder also wants this to be a UGC-marketing training ground, so formats are chosen to be **faceless and repeatable**.

Authenticity note: the builder doesn't personally believe in numerology. The content that works here is mostly *meaning-delivery* (text over aesthetic backgrounds, "what 444 means"), closer to writing horoscopes than to testimonial content, so it doesn't require performing belief. Avoid testimonial-style claims ("this changed my life").

## Content formats (all faceless-capable)

1. **Number-meaning slideshows:** "What 444 has been trying to tell you," in the brand voice, on the app's visual style.
2. **Construction-reveal screen recordings:** the daily reveal (rings trace, diamonds draw, dots settle, number lands) is a ready-made video with ~3 seconds of built-in anticipation. Caption: "POV: checking your number this morning." Needs no editing or voiceover.
3. **Comment bait:** "What number do you keep seeing? 👇" prompts generate high-volume replies that feed the algorithm.
4. **Screenshot-bait notifications:** every push notification is written as if it might be posted; share-worthy copy doubles as organic content.
5. **"You're seeing this video for a reason"** hooks and number-by-number series.

Hook structure that follows the voice: relatable observation → pointed challenge → CTA to the app for the full reading.

## Creators and budget

- Pay 5–10 micro-creators (wellness, spirituality, "that girl"/manifestation niches) **$50–$150 per video** for POV and demo videos.
- Reuse the same creatives on Reels (cross-posting keeps production cheap).
- Precedent cited from the App Mafia scene: two $50 videos reportedly seeded 200k downloads for one app (self-reported; treat as upside, not a plan).
- Beware survivorship bias and course-selling in that scene; the free podcasts and teardowns contain most of the signal.

## Funnel targets to validate before scaling spend

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Paywall view rate | ≥ 80% of installs |
| Download-to-paid | Aim for ≥ 5%; hard-paywall median is ~10.7% by day 35 |
| Red flag | < ~3% after ~1,000 installs → iterate quiz and paywall copy before buying more traffic |
| Organic UGC | If creators can't clear ~100k views cheaply, retest with a sharper identity hook |

## Risks

- **Trend fatigue:** "delulu" slang shows early fade; anchor on the durable behavior (noticing numbers, reflection), not slang.
- **Crowded edges:** many small clones in adjacent niches; first-mover branding and the distinct voice/visual system matter more than features.
- **Android:** monetizes worse for impulse subscriptions; launch iOS first.
- **Store review:** keep to wellness/self-reflection framing; no efficacy or medical claims.
- **Sharing risk:** cold copy at a vulnerable user is the viral-for-the-wrong-reason failure mode; the modulation rules in `04` are the mitigation.
