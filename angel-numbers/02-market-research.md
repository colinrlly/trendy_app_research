# 02: Market Research

## The playbook this project follows

A cluster of young founders (often called "App Mafia": Blake Anderson, Zach Yadegari, Alex Slater, others) popularized a repeatable formula for consumer subscription apps:

1. Pick an emotionally intense, identity-driven niche.
2. Build a deliberately simple app (content, quiz, tracker).
3. Pay many micro-creators ($50–$150 per video) for UGC-style videos.
4. Convert installs with an emotional onboarding quiz, a commitment moment, then a **hard paywall**.

Apps cited as examples: Cal AI, Quittr, Umax, RizzGPT/Plug AI. Their revenue numbers are mostly **founder self-reports on X and in interviews**; treat them as directional, not audited.

## Verified benchmarks (RevenueCat, State of Subscription Apps 2026)

- Hard paywalls convert roughly **5x better than freemium** (10.7% vs 2.1% download-to-paid by day 35) with nearly identical year-one retention.
- Hard-paywall apps generate roughly 8x higher revenue per install at day 60.
- About 89% of trial starts happen on **day 0**, which makes the onboarding-to-paywall sequence the whole conversion event.
- Higher-priced apps convert downloads at roughly 2x the median rate of low-priced apps.

**Correction on record:** an earlier claim that RevenueCat's data shows "spirituality" as a top-converting category was wrong. RevenueCat groups apps into broad categories and does not break out spirituality/astrology. The directional claim (mystical/identity content tolerates hard paywalls well) rests on app-level revenue estimates (Appfigures, Sensor Tower) for Co-Star, The Pattern, Nebula, Stardust, and similar. Treat it as directionally strong but unquantified.

## Why angel numbers

Scored against the rubric:

- **Impulse intensity:** the purchase is emotional ("I keep seeing 1111, what does it mean"), not utilitarian.
- **Built-in trigger loop:** numbers appear to users unprompted (clocks, receipts, plates), so the world re-engages users daily for free.
- **UGC fit:** faceless text-over-background slideshows, "what 444 means," and "what number do you keep seeing?" comment bait are proven, remixable formats.
- **Build ease:** content + quiz + daily reading.
- **Competition:** fragmented direct competitors; no app owns the angel numbers lane specifically.

### Competitive map

| Layer | Players | Why it's still open |
|---|---|---|
| Direct | A long tail of small angel-number / numerology apps | Mostly mediocre; no dominant brand |
| One scope up | Co-Star, The Pattern, Nebula, Stardust, Astrology Zone-type apps | Astrology-first; angel numbers are at most adjacent content |
| Register | Co-Star owns "blunt," but as a cold oracle | Open lane: blunt *with allegiance* (the friend, not the oracle) |

Honest caveat: this is more crowded airspace than some alternatives (e.g., the ADHD tax tracker below), and the lane is "open" in the sense of no single owner, not "empty."

## Ideas considered (so the reasoning isn't lost)

### First research pass (wellness-heavy)

Ranked: nervous-system regulation app (top pick), anxious-attachment healing app, manifestation/369-method app, breakup/no-contact tracker, shadow-work journal, sleepmaxxing ritual tracker. Rejected for incumbents: meditation (Calm/Headspace), calorie tracking (Cal AI), looksmaxxing (Umax), rizz (RizzGPT), language learning (Duolingo), period tracking (Flo/Stardust), gamified self-care (Finch), prayer (Hallow), quit-porn (Quittr), sober-curious (Sunnyside/Reframe), screen-time blockers (Opal), couples apps (Paired/Lasting).

### Brainstorm batches

- Strongest non-wellness candidates: **baby names**, **cash stuffing** (digital envelope budgeting), **interview prep**, **nail/lash business starter**.
- **ADHD niche:** the **ADHD tax tracker** was the favorite (quantified-shock onboarding, trivial build, no direct incumbent). Dopamine menu builder (charming, weak paywall justification) and room-reset app were next. Eliminated: body doubling (Dubbii, Focusmate), object finder (AirTags).
- **Interest-based:** first-time homeowner app (strong funnel fit), overland rig planner, D&D prep companion (rejected as a business; became a separate craft project).
- Eliminated for incumbents: roommate chores (Splitwise etc.), sports betting (policy minefield), plant care (Planta), MTG tools (Moxfield et al.), homelab.

### Fallback if the angel numbers content test fails

**ADHD tax tracker:** quiz tallies the user's yearly "ADHD tax" (late fees, forgotten subscriptions, impulse buys), commitment moment, hard paywall at ~$39/year. Simple build: quiz + manual logging + recovery checklist.
