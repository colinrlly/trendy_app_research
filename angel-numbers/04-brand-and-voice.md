# 04: Brand and Voice

## Positioning

**North star sentence:** *the friend who loves you too much to let you lie to yourself.*

The category splits into two registers:
- **Soft:** pastel purple, cosmic gradients, gentle affirmations.
- **Cold:** Co-Star's deadpan, ominous notifications, an oracle that doesn't care if you're listening.

This brand takes a third position: **blunt with allegiance.** Keep roughly 85% of a cold voice's punch (short, declarative, a little merciless, screenshot-shaped), but swap indifference for investment. The copy is on the user's side while being harsh.

Co-Star's coldness is not ownable IP, and copying it is allowed. It was rejected on strategy: being read as a "Co-Star clone" dampens sharing, Co-Star's coldness has drawn backlash when it lands on someone having a bad week, and the loving half is the register the builder can write honestly.

## Reflection, not prophecy

No supernatural claims. Every line works as either a sign (for believers) or a prompt for self-reflection (for skeptics). Never "the universe is telling you." Instead: "you keep noticing this; here is what it traditionally means, and here is the question it's really asking about your life."

## Voice rules (apply to every surface)

- Second person; short declarative sentences.
- Name the user's avoidance specifically but not cruelly.
- Never hedge ("the universe might be suggesting…").
- Never scold. Never use a command ("Move.").
- End on a door (a question or invitation), not a diagnosis.
- Never punch down at someone who is clearly hurting.

## Modulation: the voice must flex by number

| Level | Use | Character |
|---|---|---|
| 1: gentle | Grief and comfort numbers | Warm, steady, no push. Blunt at a grieving person is a gimmick, not a brand |
| 2: default | Most numbers; the daily card | The friend who's onto you; ends with an invitation |
| 3: max blunt | Avoidance numbers (444, 999) | Punchy and screenshot-bait, but still framed around the user's own agency |

This modulation is also a defensibility argument: knowing when *not* to push is the hardest part for a competitor to copy.

## Two-beat structure (daily readings, notifications, paywall)

**[Observation, roman] + [Challenge, italic in accent color].** The typographic split makes the modulation visible (see `05`).

## Sample copy: 11:11

**Level 1 (too soft for the default brand; reserve for comfort numbers):**
> 11:11. Everyone makes a wish. Here's a better idea: notice what you wished for. That's the part that matters, not the clock. Whatever came to mind first is what you actually want, and you just told yourself. Don't lose that.

**Level 2 (the sweet spot, used in the canonical card):**
> 11:11 again. You know the funny thing about this number? People treat it like a door about to open. But you've been standing in front of your door for a while now: hand on the handle, checking the clock instead of turning it. The alignment you keep waiting for is just you, agreeing with yourself, finally. You already agree. So what's the holdup?

**Level 3 (maximum blunt):**
> 11:11. Make a wish. No, wait. You've made this wish before. Same wish, different day, and you know exactly what it is because it's the thing you think about every time the room goes quiet. The universe isn't sending you a sign. It's sending you a receipt. You've been on the verge for months and calling it waiting.

*(The original Level 3 ended with a command, which the final rules prohibit; end on a door.)*

**Push notification:**
> It's 11:11. You thought of it immediately. That's your answer. You didn't need the clock.

**Card copy used in all mockups:**
> You've been standing in front of your door for a while now: hand on the handle, checking the clock instead of turning it.
> *You already agree with yourself. So what's the holdup?*

**444 (Level 3 example from the full-screen mock):**
> 444 keeps interrupting your day. That's not a glitch; that's a nudge to keep going.

## Funnel copy in the same voice

- Quiz: "What number keeps following you around?" / "Be honest: what were you thinking about the last time you saw it?"
- Commitment: "I'm done pretending I don't notice."
- Paywall: "You've checked what 444 means four times this week. Something's going on. Let's actually look at it."
- UI chrome carries the voice too: CTA "be honest →" instead of "Continue"; stat "Seen 4 times this week" instead of a flame-streak.

## Words and framing to avoid

"The universe is telling you," any hedge language, command-form imperatives, cosmic-whisper vocabulary (aura, vibration, divine timing), five-point stars and moon/angel-wing imagery.

## Name

Working placeholder: **"noticed."** No trademark, handle, or domain checks have been run. Naming territory explored: plainspoken English words that read like an inside reference rather than Latin-adjacent mysticism, built around the *noticing* behavior (Noticed, Seen, Again, Signal) or the *blunt friend* (Wake Up Call). An earlier brand concept "Numa" (midnight indigo, champagne-gold serif, aurora gradient, "modern celestial premium") was reviewed and set aside as the category default; it did contribute the concentric-rings-with-marked-points motif, the dark-first insight, and the tagline bones ("Decode the numbers you keep seeing").
