# Angel Numbers App: Project Handoff

A mobile angel numbers / numerology app, built solo, monetized with a hard-paywall yearly subscription, and marketed through TikTok/Instagram UGC. This folder captures every decision, piece of research, and design exploration from the planning conversation so another agent (or future-you) can pick it up without re-deriving anything.

**Working name:** "noticed" (placeholder, not trademark-checked)

## Status in one paragraph

The business model, product stance, voice, typography (Fraunces), palettes, and the daily reading card layout are settled. The daily card with the dashed circles-and-diamonds construction geometry is the canonical hero screen and the user's repeated favorite. Still open: terracotta vs plum accent, the final app name, mobile framework, and whether the celebration-burst wave ships in v1. The onboarding quiz and paywall screens have been described but never mocked visually.

## Note on conflicts between files

The appendix (`09`) was written before the later accent-color and ornament experiments. It records plum as "adopted." The user later asked to see terracotta again and has not made a final call. **Treat `05` and `08` as authoritative on the accent color: it is open.**

## What was deliberately left out

- TimeTurnip (the user's separate SaaS), the Scriptorium D&D plugin, and the other brainstormed app ideas are separate projects. Alternatives that were considered are summarized in `02` only so the reasoning isn't lost.
- Revenue figures quoted in `02` are mostly founder self-reports or modeled estimates. They are directional, not audited.
